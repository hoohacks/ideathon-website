About

Why the Ideathon exists.

Plenty of UVA students have the technical skill to build a product. Plenty have the business skill to launch one. Very few have both. The Ideathon brings the two groups together for a day to bridge that gap and help foster entrepreneurship at UVA.

  • A team

    Technical students match with business students. Bring an idea and find people to build it, or bring your skills and join an idea that needs them.

  • A mentor

    UVA faculty and working professionals hold office hours through the day. Teams meet them for twenty or thirty minutes, swap contacts, and stay in touch after the event.

  • A plan

    Workshops from UVA faculty and industry professionals on digital innovation and marketing. You leave with a market plan and a build outline for your idea.

  • A pitch

    Present at the expo and to a panel of professionals. Teams compete for feedback and for seed money to build the idea.

FAQ

Common questions.

Why should students come? What do they get out of it?

A mentor and point of contact, and a team for their idea. An understanding of what a hackathon is, and experience in both commerce and computer science. A market plan and a build outline for their application, free lunch, and potentially extra credit for their classes. Teams also have the opportunity to win seed money to bring their idea to life.

What should students bring?

Ideas, if they have them.

What is expected of mentors?

We would love for mentors to participate for a minimum of two hours, and welcome them to stay longer. We would also love to have mentors give talks or run workshops if they are interested. We expect mentors to have twenty to thirty minute meetings with each team they mentor, and to maintain however much contact teams would like after the Ideathon.

How are mentors assigned?

We do not assign mentors. Mentors sit in Davis Commons where teams come and ask questions, office hours style. Mentors are welcome to walk around to individual teams if they prefer.

What do sponsors get out of sponsoring the Ideathon?

Sponsors meet UVA students who are passionate about a good idea and capable of executing on it. They also build relationships with UVA clubs, especially the Virginia Venture Fund and HooHacks, and help foster entrepreneurship at UVA. Contact us for more information about sponsoring.

What do you want from sponsors?

We would like sponsors to provide mentors and speakers to give talks or run workshops. We would also love it if attendees could form connections with sponsors for internships and other learning opportunities.
